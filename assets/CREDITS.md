# Asset credits

CHOWK's original meshes and textures use the repository's [MIT licence](../LICENSE). The vehicle models come from `assets/blender/vehicles.py`. The low-detail vehicles, buildings and street props come from `assets/blender/district.py`. The placeholder generator uses `assets/blender/placeholders.py`.

The authored network defines the roads, kerbs and markings. The scene generates window lights, lamp pools and skyline placement in code. The poster, screenshots and recordings come from the running application.
